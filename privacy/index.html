Privacy

StackPicks has no accounts, no logins and no sign-up wall, does not run advertising trackers, and does not sell personal data. Last updated October 9, 2026.

Analytics. We count visits with Umami, an open-source analytics tool that we host ourselves on a server we operate, not a third-party analytics service. For each page view it records the page, the site you came from, your browser and device type, screen size, language and approximate country, and it records which product buttons are clicked. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information, so no cookie banner is needed. We look at the totals to see which guides are read, never at individual visitors.

Hosting and fonts. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and browser details in its server logs; GitHub's privacy statement applies to that. Pages also load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, and Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Affiliate links. Buttons such as “Check price” take you to a retailer. A paid link carries our affiliate identifier so the retailer can credit a purchase to us. Once you arrive, the retailer (Amazon, for example) may set its own cookies and its own privacy policy applies. The affiliate programs report clicks, orders and commissions to us in aggregate; we never receive your name, address, payment details or what else was in your cart. How we earn money is explained on the affiliate disclosure page.

Campaign tags. Links we share on social platforms such as Pinterest may carry campaign tags (for example utm_source) so we can count visits by source. The tags contain no personal information.

Our Pinterest app. We publish our own Pins to our own Pinterest account (@stackpicksmedia) through Postiz, a scheduling tool we host ourselves, connected to the Pinterest API as the app “StackPicks Postiz”. It is used by one account only, ours, and is not offered to anyone else. It requests access to our own boards and Pins so it can list our boards and create and schedule Pins on them. The only Pinterest data it keeps is the access token Pinterest issues for our account and the Pins we schedule; the token is stored only on our own server, never shared or sold, and is deleted if we disconnect the app. It does not collect, store or analyze any other Pinterest user’s data, and it is not used for advertising. To ask about it, email us at the address below.

Email and children. If you email us we keep the message so we can reply to it, and for nothing else. Contact: stackpicksmedia@gmail.com. This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because we collect no personally identifiable information there is normally nothing to access or delete; if you think we hold something about you, email us and we will look.
