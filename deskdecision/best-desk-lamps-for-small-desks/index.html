The right question for a small desk is not how bright a lamp is, it is where it stands. A base lamp puts a 7 by 14-inch footprint on a worktop that may only be 15 inches deep, in the corner you wanted for a notebook. Three lamps here clip to the monitor and take no surface at all; two clamp to the desk edge; one has a base and earns it back with a charging pad.

There is a catch nobody mentions: monitor bars publish lux at a stated distance, conventional lamps publish lumens, and the two cannot be ranked against each other. The table prints each maker's own figure in its own unit rather than pretending otherwise. Spend as little as possible and the IKEA NÄVLINGE is 220 lumens on a clamp for pocket money.

How to shop for a desk lamp for a small desk

How much desk it occupies
The reason the page exists. A bar takes none, a clamp about two square inches of edge, a base lamp its whole footprint out of a surface you do not have.
Published light output, in the maker's own unit
BenQ publishes 930 lux at 45 cm. IKEA publishes 220 and 370 lumens. Mount-It! publishes lux without a distance on two of three. Compare within a unit, never across.
Whether it throws light at the screen
A lamp beside a monitor reflects off the panel, which is the glare that makes an evening feel long. Bars use an asymmetric reflector that throws light down at the desk instead.
Color temperature range
Anything fixed at one temperature is a lamp for one time of day. Mount-It!'s three run 3000 to 6500 K; IKEA's NÄVLINGE is fixed at 2700 K warm white.
How it is powered
A USB bar draws from the monitor and adds no plug to the room. A mains lamp adds another brick to whatever your under-desk cable tray is already holding.

Questions people ask

How much light does a desk actually need?

BenQ builds its auto-dimming around 500 lux at the desk, which it cites as the American National Standard figure for office lighting, and publishes the lit area at that level — 60 by 30 cm for the ScreenBar. Compare against that, not a peak center figure.

Do monitor light bars cause screen glare?

Not if they are built correctly. The reflector inside a bar is asymmetric on purpose, throwing light forward and down rather than back at the panel — the feature that separates a bar from a lamp balanced on a monitor.

Will a light bar fit my monitor?

Check the published thickness range. BenQ rates the ScreenBar for panels 1 to 3 cm thick. Neither Mount-It! nor IKEA publishes a range for theirs, so on those two it is a question you answer on arrival.

Lux or lumens — which should I compare?

Only within the same unit, and only when both quote a measuring distance. BenQ's 930 lux at 45 cm and Mount-It!'s over 700 lux at 400 mm can be read against each other; over 1700 lux with no distance cannot.

Is a clamp lamp better than a base lamp on a small desk?

Almost always. A clamp takes about two square inches of edge; the base lamp here takes 14 by 7.2 inches, roughly a sixth of a 43 by 15-inch desk.

Does warm or cool light matter for work?

It matters for when you use it. Four of six adjust between roughly 3000 and 6500 K. The IKEA clamp spotlight is fixed at 2700 K — pleasant at night, flat for detail work.
