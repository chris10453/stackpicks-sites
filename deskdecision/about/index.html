About Desk Decision

Desk Decision is a StackPicks desk, published from Florida. It covers one situation: a home office that is a corner of a room rather than a room, where the binding constraint is almost never money and almost always inches — the depth of a desk, the reach of a monitor arm, the clearance under a worktop, the distance from your eyes to a screen.

Every specification printed here is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page or manual, and every one of those pages is linked at the bottom of the guide that uses it. Where two of a maker's own pages disagree, we print both figures and say so. Where a maker publishes nothing, the cell reads "not published" rather than a plausible-looking number, because an invented dimension is worse than a gap.

We earn a commission when you buy through a link on this site (see the affiliate disclosure). Brands and retailers do not pay to be included, cannot see a guide before it is published, and cannot change a ranking. We do not print prices, because retailers change them hourly and a stale number is worse than none; the price band on each pick describes where a product sits relative to the others on that page on the day it was checked.
