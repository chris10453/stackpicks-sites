OUR METHOD

Useful beats impressive.

Desk Decision exists to help a reader make a smaller, better-informed purchase—not to manufacture a winner for every category.

What we check

  1. Fit: What job does the product actually address? We do not compare unrelated products as if they were substitutes.
  2. Visible facts: We record dimensions, compatibility, listed features, price snapshots, and availability only when a public source supports them.
  3. Tradeoffs: Every record gets a “watch for” section. A useful recommendation includes the reason not to buy.
  4. Source date: Retail price, seller, stock, and delivery information can change. The live listing is the purchase source.
  5. Claims boundary: We do not claim medical benefit, ergonomic certification, guaranteed productivity, or first-hand testing unless we can prove it.

What we do not do

We do not use star ratings as our editorial judgment, copy testimonials from product listings as independent proof, or fill a comparison table with products that do not compete. We do not add an email form until a real consent-and-storage backend exists.

How the site earns

Some links are affiliate links. If a reader buys after clicking one, the site may receive a commission. That does not change the research boundary or guarantee a product is right for a particular person.
