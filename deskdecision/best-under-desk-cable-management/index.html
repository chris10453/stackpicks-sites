Cable management in a small space is a different problem from cable management in an office. There is no cupboard to hide the brick in, the desk is visible from the bed or the couch, and anything bolted under it may hit your knees. The specification that matters is not capacity — every tray here holds 11 lb — it is where the thing fixes and how far it hangs.

If you rent and cannot drill, the Mount-It! clamp-on tray takes tops from 0.2 to 1.97 inches with no screws at all. If your desk goes up and down, the Branch Cable Snake is the one designed to move with it. Every dimension below is quoted from the maker's own page.

How to shop for under-desk cable management

How it fixes to the desk
Screws hold more and cost a rented desk's deposit. Clamps come off. Three of six screw in, one clamps, one sits on the floor, one bolts into threaded inserts.
Under-desk clearance
VIVO publishes 6.5 inches of under-desk clearance for its flip-open box and 7.2 for the lid to swing. On a 29-inch desk that leaves 22 inches of knee height.
Whether it has a lid
An open tray is faster to work in; a closed box hides the mess completely. Only two here close, and only one opens without unplugging everything.
Minimum desk thickness
The thing nobody checks. VIVO's flip-open box needs 0.6 inches of top; Mount-It!'s clamp works from 0.2. A 15 mm laminate top rules some of these out.
Whether it follows a sit-stand desk
A desk that travels 18 inches drags its cables with it, and a fixed tray plus a taut lead is how a sit-stand desk unplugs itself.

Questions people ask

How much weight does a cable tray actually need to hold?

Less than you think. A six-outlet surge protector is about 1.5 lb, a laptop charger about 0.8, a monitor brick under a pound. The 11 lb the VIVO and Mount-It! trays publish is roughly four times a normal load.

Can I fit any of these without drilling?

Two. The Mount-It! clamp-on tray grips tops from 0.2 to 1.97 inches with thumbscrews and leaves no mark, and the Bluelounge CableBox sits on the floor and attaches to nothing. Everything else on this page screws or bolts into the desk.

Will a tray hit my knees?

Subtract the tray's height from your desk height and compare with your seated knee height. At 4.5 inches the VIVO tray leaves about 24.5 inches under a 29-inch desk; the 7-inch Mount-It! leaves about 22.

What about a desk that goes up and down?

Fix the tray to the desktop so it travels with the desk, run the bundle down a spine like the Branch Cable Snake, and leave slack at the wall for the full travel.

Are adhesive cable channels worth it?

Only for the short runs a tray cannot reach, and even then the adhesive is the failure point in a warm room. Anything carrying a power strip's weight should be mechanically fixed — screwed, clamped or bolted.

Do I need a closed box or is a tray enough?

A tray is enough if nobody looks under the desk. A closed box earns the extra in a studio where the desk is visible from the sofa, because it hides the plugs as well as the slack.

  • Adhesive-backed cable raceway — Fine for a short wall run, wrong under a desk where the adhesive holds weight in the warmest air in the room.
