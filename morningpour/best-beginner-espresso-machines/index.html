Two other honest answers. The De'Longhi Stilosa is the cheapest route to a real portafilter and a real steam wand, and the Gaggia Classic Pro is the one you will still be using in five years — the only machine here whose manufacturer publishes brew pressure rather than pump pressure.

Which is the thing every beginner espresso page gets wrong. The "15 bar" or "20 bar" on the box is the pump's maximum against a closed outlet with no coffee in the way; real espresso extracts at around 9 bar. Gaggia publishes both figures — a 15-bar Ulka pump with the relief valve calibrated to 9 bar. How to shop for a beginner espresso machine

Heat-up time
The spec that decides whether you use the machine on a weekday. Published times here run from 3 seconds to 2 minutes, and one machine has no heater at all.
Pressurized or non-pressurized baskets
A pressurized basket makes drinkable espresso from any grind and hides your mistakes; a single-wall basket demands a real burr grinder and teaches you. Five of these six have no grinder, and espresso is the one brew method where pre-ground coffee will not work.
Portafilter diameter
54 mm and 58 mm have accessories everywhere, 51 mm has fewer, 40 mm is a lever standard. It decides whether you can buy a better basket later.
Footprint and water tank
These machines live out permanently. Published widths here run from 5.7 to 15.9 inches and tanks from a 60 mL brew cylinder to 72 oz.

Questions people ask

Do I need a grinder to use an espresso machine?

Yes, and it is not optional on five of these six. Espresso needs a grind fine enough to build resistance and adjustable in very small steps. Only the pressurized-basket machines tolerate a mediocre grind, and even they are limited by it.

Is a 20 bar pump better than a 15 bar pump?

No. Both are the pump's maximum against a closed outlet with no coffee in the way. Espresso extracts at around 9 bar once there is a puck and a relief valve in the circuit, and Gaggia is the only manufacturer here that publishes its 9-bar calibration.

What is the difference between pressurized and non-pressurized baskets?

A pressurized basket has a restrictor that creates back-pressure mechanically, so it makes crema from almost any grind. A single-wall basket makes the coffee provide the resistance, which needs a proper grinder. The Dedica Duo and Flair use single-wall, the Stilosa and Casabrews pressurized, and the Bambino ships with both.

How much counter space does a home espresso machine need?

Less than you think, if you shop on width: the Casabrews is 5.7 inches wide and the Dedica Duo 5.9. The Gaggia is 8 inches wide but 14.2 inches tall, which is the one to check against your cabinet clearance.

Can I make a latte on all of these?

On five of them. Every electric machine here has a manual steam wand, so you texture the milk yourself. The Flair Classic has none and needs a separate frother.
