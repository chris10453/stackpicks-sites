The home coffee guide for the kitchen you actually have

Most coffee gear reviews are written by people who taste. This one starts earlier, with the question that actually stops the purchase: does it fit? A machine that brews beautifully and stands 15.8 inches tall is useless under a cabinet that leaves you 15.5 inches. Every guide here leads with height, footprint and the numbers the manufacturer publishes, and only then argues about the coffee.

If you want the short version: the pour-over guide is the flagship, because a gooseneck kettle is the single upgrade that changes the most cups for the least money. If your counter is the problem, start with the small-kitchen drip guide.

We do not print prices, quote star scores, or claim to have run a lab. We read the spec sheets, we link every one of them, and we name what we rejected.

What this site promises, and what it does not

  • Every spec number is linked to the manufacturer's own page. Open the Sources block at the bottom of any guide and check one. If a brand does not publish a figure, the guide says "not published" rather than filling the gap.
  • No prices, no star scores, no review counts. Retailers change prices; a number typed into a page by hand is wrong within a week. Every guide uses a price band and hands you off to the retailer for the real figure.
  • Zero sponsored placements. No brand has paid for a position here, and no brand sees a guide before it publishes.
  • The rejects are named. Every guide ends with the products that did not make it and the specific number that disqualified them.

How we pick

Each guide starts with a constraint a buyer can measure — cabinet clearance, counter width, a grind range, a temperature — and ranks against it. Specifications come from the manufacturer's published documentation, converted into the units a kitchen is built in. We do not run a lab and we never write "we tested" about something we did not do; where a claim comes from published documentation, the guide says so, and the source is listed. This is research, done carefully, with the sources in the open.
