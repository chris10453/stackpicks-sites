How to shop for a moka pot

How much coffee it actually makes
This is the number the category hides behind the word "cup". Bialetti publishes a full conversion — 2 oz per cup — on its own pages, and the GROSCHE MILANO's retail page gives the six-cup as 9.3 oz. One pick here carries no ounce figure at all, and that is recorded in the table rather than guessed at.
Whether it works on your stove
Aluminum is not magnetic, so a classic moka pot does nothing on an induction hob. Bialetti sells an adapter plate; three pots here have a steel boiler and need no adapter at all. Check what your kitchen has before you check anything else.
The handle and the safety valve
A moka pot is a small pressure vessel with a hot metal handle. GROSCHE publishes a certified Italian safety valve and a burn guard built into the handle; Bialetti has had a patented safety valve since long before it was fashionable. This is the part of the specification that is about your hand.
Aluminum or stainless
Aluminum conducts heat faster, costs far less and must be hand washed with water alone — no detergent, no dishwasher, because the seasoned layer inside is doing real work. Stainless can be washed properly, survives a dishwasher and costs two to three times as much.
What you can replace
Gaskets perish and filter plates clog. GROSCHE and Cuisinox both sell gaskets, funnels and filter plates as separate parts by size, and Cuisinox sells a replacement bottom boiler. A moka pot should be a twenty-year object, and it only is if the rubber ring is a catalog item.

Questions people ask

How many ounces does a 6-cup moka pot make?

Bialetti publishes 9.1 oz for its six-cup and GROSCHE publishes 9.3 oz for the aluminum MILANO. That is one large mug or two small ones. The whole scale, from Bialetti's own page, runs 1 cup = 2 oz, 3 cup = 4.4 oz, 6 cup = 9.1 oz, 9 cup = 14.2 oz, 12 cup = 22 oz and 18 cup = 27.3 oz.

Is moka pot coffee actually espresso?

No. A moka pot brews at a few bars of pressure against the nine a pump machine uses, so there is no real crema and the extraction is different. It is its own drink — strong, syrupy, closer to espresso than to drip. If you want espresso proper, our beginner espresso guide covers the machines that get there.

Will a moka pot work on an induction hob?

Only if the boiler is steel. Aluminum is not magnetic, so the classic pots do nothing on induction unless you buy an adapter plate. Three picks here need no adapter: the Bialetti Moka Induction and the GROSCHE MILANO Steel both put a steel boiler under an aluminum top, and the Cuisinox Roma is steel throughout.

Why should I not use soap on an aluminum moka pot?

Detergent strips the thin oil layer that builds up inside an aluminum pot, and that layer is part of why the coffee stops tasting metallic after the first few brews. Bialetti's own instruction is warm water only. A stainless pot like the Cuisinox has no such layer and washes like any other pan.

What grind should I use in a moka pot?

Finer than drip and coarser than espresso, and never tamped — fill the funnel level and knock it flat. Too fine and the pressure builds until the safety valve is the only way out; too coarse and the water runs through sour. Our grind size chart has the maker figures.
