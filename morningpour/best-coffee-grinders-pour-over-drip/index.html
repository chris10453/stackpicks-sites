The grinder decides more about a cup of drip or pour-over than the brewer does, and it is the piece almost everyone buys last. Every pick here is a burr grinder on purpose; if you are still deciding whether that matters, read burr vs blade first.

How to shop for a coffee grinder

Burr type and diameter
Bigger burrs cut more beans per rotation at lower speed — less heat, a narrower spread of particle sizes. The published range runs from a 38 mm hand burr to 64 mm flats, and three brands decline to publish a diameter at all.
Settings you can return to
Stepped settings are repeatable — you can go back to 18 tomorrow. Stepless is finer, but you are relying on a mark you made yourself. For drip and pour-over, repeatable beats fine.
Hopper or single dose, and cleaning
A hopper holds a week of beans and stales them; a single-dose bin means you weigh what you need. Quick-release burrs and removable bins are what make a grinder cleanable in practice.
Height, because of the cabinet
A standard cabinet leaves about 18 inches of clearance and the hopper lid still has to open under it. The Breville is 15.3 inches tall before that lid moves.

Questions people ask

What grind setting should I use for pour-over?

Middle of the dial — roughly 18 to 22 of 40 on a Baratza Encore, coarser than espresso and finer than French press. If the brew runs through in under two minutes it is too coarse.

Does burr size actually matter?

Yes, and it is the specification to compare. Larger burrs cut more beans per rotation — fewer rotations, less heat, a narrower spread of particle sizes. The range here runs from 38 mm on the Timemore to 64 mm flats on the Fellow Ode.

Conical or flat burrs for filter coffee?

Flat burrs tend to produce a tighter particle distribution, which is why the Ode's 64 mm flats are the pour-over pick. Conical burrs are cheaper and run cooler at low speed, like the Encore's 40 mm set. For drip, either is far beyond a blade grinder.

Is a hand grinder good enough for daily coffee?

For one or two cups, easily. The Timemore C3 ESP adjusts in 0.0233 mm clicks, finer than any electric here, and costs less. The limit is volume and effort: 20 grams per grind and 30 to 60 seconds of cranking each time.

How tall a grinder will fit under my kitchen cabinet?

Under a standard 18-inch clearance, aim for 13 inches or less so the hopper still opens. The Fellow Opus 2 is 10.5 inches, the Encore is 34 cm or about 13.4, and the Breville at 15.3 has to be pulled forward to fill.
