A spray ceramic is not a real coating and does not pretend to be. What it is, is a wax replacement that beads harder, lasts months rather than weeks and takes about twenty minutes on a washed car. If you have never used one, the cheapest bottle teaches you the technique without punishing a mistake.

The numbers worth comparing are durability and how thin you can spread it. Chemical Guys publishes up to 12 months for HydroCharge+; Adam's publishes contact angles of 110 to 118 degrees and a sliding angle under 15 degrees for its Graphene spray; Turtle Wax publishes the panel size and the cure time. Everything below comes from those pages, linked at the bottom.

How to shop for a ceramic spray coating

Published durability
Chemical Guys states up to 12 months for HydroCharge+ and up to a year for HydroSlick; Adam's states 1+ years for its graphene spray. Where a brand publishes nothing, treat it as a seasonal product.
Contact angle, where it is published
Contact angle is how tightly water balls up. Adam's publishes 110 to 118 degrees and a sliding angle under 10 to 15 degrees for the graphene spray — the only measured hydrophobicity figures in this category.
How forgiving the application is
A beginner's coating has to tolerate being left a minute too long. Spray-and-wipe products worked 2 x 2 feet at a time, like the Turtle Wax, forgive that; gels and true coatings do not.
Bottle size against the job
A sedan takes a few ounces a coat. Sizes here run 12 oz on the Adam's graphene spray, 16 oz on the Turtle Wax and Chemical Guys bottles, 22 oz on the Griot's and 24 oz on the Mothers CMX.
Whether it can top an existing coating
HydroCharge+ and CMX are both sold as boosters over an existing coating or sealant, which makes them useful later even if you move to a professional coating.
What it does to your wash routine
Coated paint is easier to wash but hates strong degreasers. Plan on a pH-neutral shampoo afterwards — the wheel cleaners and all-purpose sprays that strip road film also strip a spray ceramic early.

Questions people ask

How long does a ceramic spray coating actually last?

The published claims are up to 12 months for Chemical Guys HydroCharge+ and 1+ years for Adam's graphene spray, both on cars that are washed properly and kept out of harsh chemicals. On a car parked outside and washed at a coin wash, plan on a top-up every few months and judge by the beading.

Do I need to polish the paint first?

For the best result, yes — a coating locks in whatever is underneath, including swirl marks. For a first attempt, a thorough wash, a decontamination step and a clean dry surface are enough. Polishing is the next project, not a prerequisite.

What is contact angle and does it matter?

It is the angle a water droplet makes with the paint. Higher means tighter beads: Adam's publishes 110 to 118 degrees for its graphene spray. The more useful number is the sliding angle — under 10 to 15 degrees means water runs off a gently sloped panel instead of sitting there and spotting.

Can I put a ceramic spray over wax?

No. A coating needs to bond to clean paint, and a layer of carnauba is exactly what stops that. Strip first with a dedicated prep spray or a strong wash, then coat. The reverse — a spray ceramic first, wax on top — is pointless but harmless.

How much do I use on one car?

A few ounces per coat on a sedan, applied two sprays at a time to a 2 x 2 foot section as Turtle Wax directs. A 16 oz bottle is several full applications, which is why the 24 oz Mothers bottle only makes sense for more than one car.

Also considered

  • "9H" coatings sold only through marketplaces — 9H describes a pencil hardness test on a cured film, not on your paint, and a seller with no product page has published nothing to check. The hardness number in this category is decoration.
  • Meguiar's Hybrid Ceramic Wax — A popular product we could not include honestly — the manufacturer's site did not respond to any automated request while this guide was written, so there was no maker page to quote specifications from.
  • Spray waxes labelled ceramic with no SiO2 claim — If the page will not say what the chemistry is, the word ceramic on the front is marketing. Every product above at least names its chemistry.
  • Two-part professional coating kits — Genuinely more durable and genuinely unforgiving. High spots that are not levelled within minutes need machine polishing to remove, which is not a beginner's first job.
  • Ceramic-infused car wash soaps as a substitute — They add a little protection during a wash, which is a nice extra and not a coating. Use one after you have coated the car, not instead.
