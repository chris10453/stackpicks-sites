How to shop for a first car wash kit

Two buckets, or don't bother
One bucket means every stroke after the first reloads the mitt with the dirt you just removed. A wash bucket, a rinse bucket and a grit screen in the rinse bucket is the entire anti-swirl system.
pH-balanced soap, not dish detergent
Dish soap strips wax and sealant along with the road film. A pH-balanced car shampoo is formulated to clean and leave the protection in place, which matters if you ever want the car to bead water again.
Microfiber that releases dirt
A sponge holds grit in its cells and drags it across the clear coat. A deep-pile microfiber mitt drops what it picks up when you agitate it in the rinse bucket. Edgeless, so no stitched seam touches paint.
A drying towel that does a panel in one pass
Water spots form where a panel air-dries, so drying speed is a paint issue. A 20 × 30-inch, 900 gsm towel takes a full door in one pull; a 16-inch towel needs four and saturates halfway through.
Cost per wash, not price on the box
A 64 oz bottle of concentrate diluted into a 5-gallon bucket outlasts a 16 oz pre-mixed bottle by a season. Judge soap by washes covered, towels by whether the brand still sells replacements in five years.
Wheels get their own bucket and chemical
Brake dust is metal, and whatever cleans wheels should never touch paint afterwards — which is why the wheel cleaner here is spray-on, hose-off.

Questions people ask

How many buckets do I actually need?

Three, and they cost about what one bottle of soap costs: a wash bucket with soap, a rinse bucket with a 12-inch grit screen, and one for wheels that never touches paint. Two is the working minimum; one bucket is what causes the swirl marks you are trying to avoid.

Can I use dish soap instead of car shampoo?

Once, and it will strip whatever wax or sealant is on the car. A pH-balanced shampoo leaves that protection in place, and a 64 fl oz bottle of concentrate covers a season of weekly washes — so the saving from dish soap is a couple of dollars a year.

What size drying towel should I buy?

One 20 × 30 inch, 900 gsm towel dries a full-size sedan if you fold it into eight sides. Anything under about 16 × 16 inches saturates on the roof and hood, which is where water spots form first.

How do I wash the microfiber?

Machine wash separately from cotton, no fabric softener, dry on low or hang dry. Softener coats the fibers and kills absorbency — a 900 gsm towel washed with softener dries about as well as a bath towel, which is the one thing it must not do.
