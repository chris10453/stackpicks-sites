Questions people ask

What is the best gift for someone who is just getting into detailing?

The swirl-finder flashlight, oddly. It is the cheapest thing on this page and the only one that changes how somebody sees their own car. Everything else is a tool for a problem they have not noticed yet.

Is a dual-action polisher safe for a beginner?

Dual-action machines are the beginner-safe category: the head oscillates as well as rotates and will not burn through paint the way a rotary can. A 15 mm throw is a general-purpose size. Pads and compound are a separate purchase unless you buy a kit version.

Do they need a special electrical circuit for the air dryer?

Adam's specifies a 20 amp circuit for the Air Cannon. A standard 15 amp garage circuit trips under a dual-motor 3.2 HP load, so check the breaker before buying this as a surprise.

What does CRI mean on a detailing light?

Color rendering index, on a scale to 100, describing how accurately a light shows color against daylight. Adam's publishes 95 CRI for the Color Match light, and high CRI is what makes fine marring visible in a reflection rather than just making the panel bright.

Are microfiber towels a lazy gift?

Only if you buy unbranded ones. A published GSM and size — 480 GSM at 16 × 16 in here — puts a towel in a specific place in someone's collection, and three colors solves the real problem of using the wheel towel on the paint.

What should I avoid buying a home detailer?

Supermarket kits, anything with no published specification, and any chemical — they already have opinions about chemicals. Tools, lights and storage are the safe categories.

Also considered

  • Boxed car care gift sets — Small bottles of products the person already buys in larger sizes, at a premium. The piece count is the only figure published, and piece count is not a specification.
  • Novelty air fresheners and dash accessories — Somebody who details their own car has strong feelings about what hangs from the mirror. The answer is usually nothing.
  • Chemicals and shampoos — The one category where they have already chosen. A different soap is a suggestion rather than a gift, and the half-used bottle sits on the cart forever.
  • Cheap rotary polishers — A rotary in inexperienced hands takes clear coat off an edge in seconds. Dual-action is the category to buy in; budget rotaries turn a gift into a repair bill.
