About The Wash Bay

The Wash Bay is an independent car-care guide for people who wash at home: a driveway, a hose, a couple of buckets, and no commercial bay. The guides are written by the StackPicks editorial team.

The comparisons on this site are built from things a buyer can check: tank volume in gallons, air flow in CFM, maximum working pressure in PSI, towel weight in GSM, duty cycle in minutes, what the warranty actually covers, and whether you can buy a replacement filter two years from now. Every number in a spec block comes from the manufacturer's own product page, spec sheet or manual — never from a retailer's copy — and the sources are listed at the bottom of each guide.

What you will not find: star scores copied from somewhere else, fake head-to-head tests, prices typed by hand (retailers change them hourly), or a page where every product mentioned is a winner. Each guide names what we rejected and why.

The Wash Bay earns a commission when you buy through a link here — see the affiliate disclosure. No brand pays for placement, sees a guide before it publishes, or can change a ranking. Corrections and disagreements are welcome: stackpicksmedia@gmail.com.

Who writes this

The Wash Bay is written by the StackPicks editorial team and published under the house byline “StackPicks Editors”. That byline is a pen name for the team, not one named person. StackPicks is written by its editorial team; product research is desk-based, from manufacturer specs, manuals and other documented sources. We do not present desk research as hands-on lab testing unless a guide says so, and a guide says so only where the product was actually bought and used.

The “StackPicks score” and the awards on a guide are editorial judgments built from the published figures shown on that page. They are not laboratory results, and they are not copied from retailer star ratings. Safety, structural and veterinary questions are outside what this site can judge: where a guide touches one, it says so and points to the manufacturer, a qualified professional or your veterinarian.
