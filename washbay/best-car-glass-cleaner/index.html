How to shop for a car glass cleaner

Ammonia, stated in writing
Ammonia damages dyed aftermarket window film and hazes it over time. Stoner, Chemical Guys and Mothers all state ammonia-free in their own copy. One product in this guide does not state it either way, and on the inside of a tinted window that silence is the deciding factor.
Whether the maker says it is tint-safe
Ammonia-free and tint-safe are related but not identical claims, and the makers who mean it print both. Five of the six print a flat tint-safe claim; the sixth prints a warning to test it on the film first, which is the opposite of a clearance.
Bottle size against a whole car
A windshield inside and out, four side windows, a rear screen and two mirrors is more glass than people expect. Bottles here run 16 to 24 fluid ounces, and the cheapest bottle in the guide is also the second largest.
What else the cleaner is rated for
Mothers rates Revision for glass, mirrors, plastic, chrome, gauges, touchscreens and phones. Adam's rates its cleaner for tinted windows and tablet screens. A glass cleaner that is also a screen cleaner saves a second bottle in the door pocket.
Delivery — trigger, foam or aerosol
A foaming aerosol clings to vertical glass instead of running down onto the door card. Mothers publishes that its Speed Foaming version will not drip or run, which is a real advantage on a windshield's interior slope.
Whether it leaves anything behind
Chemical Guys HydroView is the only product here that deliberately leaves something on the glass — a hydrophobic ceramic layer sold as lasting up to 12 months. Everything else is trying to leave nothing at all, and Stoner says its formula is tested residue-free by independent laboratories.

Questions people ask

Why does ammonia matter in a car glass cleaner?

Aftermarket window film is a dyed polyester laminate held on with adhesive, and ammonia degrades both. It also dries the rubber and vinyl around the glass. Stoner, Mothers and Chemical Guys all state ammonia-free on the products in this guide; household blue glass cleaner generally does not.

What actually causes streaks on car glass?

Usually the cleaner rather than the dirt. Soaps, scents and dyes dry into a film that shows up in low sun, which is why Stoner sells Invisible Glass on containing none of them. The second cause is a towel that has met fabric softener, which coats the fibers and smears.

What towel should I use on glass?

A dedicated waffle-weave or glass-specific microfiber, washed without fabric softener, and two of them — one damp to clean and one dry to buff. The buff pass is what removes the last of the cleaner, and doing it with the same towel just moves the film around.

How do I clean the inside of a windshield?

Spray the towel rather than the glass, work in one direction, and use a foaming product if you have one — Mothers publishes that its Speed Foaming version will not drip or run, which is the whole problem with liquids on a raked windshield. Then buff dry with a second towel in the opposite direction, so you can tell which side a streak is on.

Do glass coatings actually help?

In heavy rain at speed, yes — water beads and clears without the wipers. Chemical Guys sells HydroView as lasting up to 12 months, the only durability figure published in this guide. In light rain or at low speed the effect is much less noticeable, and some people find wipers judder on coated glass.

Also considered

  • Household blue glass cleaner — Cheap, effective on kitchen windows and generally ammonia-based, which is the one chemistry that damages aftermarket tint. The dyes are also what the automotive products have deliberately removed.
  • Glass cleaning wipes sold in tubs — Convenient in a door pocket and expensive per window. They also dry out once the seal is broken, so the last third of the tub is doing nothing useful.
  • Vinegar and newspaper — Vinegar is acidic and modern newsprint inks are not the ones this advice was written for. On a car it also finds its way into the window seals and the door card, which is where a cleaner rated for plastic and chrome is worth the money.
  • Glass cleaners with no published ammonia statement and no tint claim — On the inside of a tinted rear screen those are the only two facts that matter. A seller unwilling to print either has told you enough.
