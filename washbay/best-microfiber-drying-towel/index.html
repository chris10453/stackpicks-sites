Drying is where most home washes go wrong. The wash was careful, the rinse was thorough, and then a bath towel or a two-year-old chamois drags the last of the grit across a dry panel. A large, heavy, soft towel is the cheapest insurance on this site: one item, one purchase, and the swirl marks stop appearing.

Three numbers decide a drying towel and two of them are usually missing from the box: GSM, dimensions, and the weave. Everything below is compared on the manufacturer's own published figures — 900 gsm and 20 x 30 on the Gauntlet, 830 gsm and 33 x 29 on the Adam's Jumbo Plush, 24 x 36 on the Megalodon — with the sources linked at the bottom.

How to shop for a microfiber drying towel

Published GSM
Grams per square meter is how much fiber is actually there. The Rag Company publishes 900 gsm for the Gauntlet and Adam's publishes 830 gsm for the Jumbo Plush; brands that publish nothing are asking you to judge a towel by its photograph.
Size against one panel
A towel should cross a door or a third of a roof in one pass. That means at least 20 x 30 inches, and the big ones here run to 33 x 29 and 29 x 36. Small towels mean more passes, and passes are where scratches come from.
Weave, not just thickness
Twisted loop wicks fast and grips less; waffle weave holds a lot of water and glides on glass; plush is gentlest and slowest. The Gauntlet stripes twist loop against plush deliberately, and that is why it is the pick.
The border
A stitched polyester border is the hardest thing on the towel and the part that reaches paint first. Suede or edgeless borders — the Gauntlet's ButterSoft suede, Griot's edgeless construction — remove that risk entirely.
How much water it actually holds
The Rag Company says Dry Me A River soaks up 10 times its weight; Chemical Guys says the Megalodon holds more than a gallon. Those are the only two absorbency claims in this category with a number attached.
Whether it survives the laundry
A drying towel is washed after every use. Split microfiber survives that; cheap blends mat down and start skating. Both Rag Company towels are Korean split microfiber, which is the specification worth paying for.

Questions people ask

What GSM should a car drying towel be?

For a dedicated drying towel, 800 gsm and up. The Gauntlet is 900 gsm and Adam's Jumbo Plush is 830 gsm. Below about 500 gsm you are into general-purpose and glass towels, which is what a waffle weave like Dry Me A River is for.

How many drying towels do I need for one car?

One large towel of 20 x 30 inches or bigger for paint, plus one waffle weave for glass and door jambs. On an SUV, either a second large towel or a 24 x 36 that holds a gallon, like the Megalodon.

Twisted loop or waffle weave — which dries better?

Twisted loop moves standing water faster and is the better first pass on paint. Waffle weave holds more water for its thickness and leaves glass streak-free, which is why most setups end up owning both.

How do I wash a microfiber drying towel?

Warm wash with a microfiber detergent, no fabric softener ever, and low or no heat in the dryer. Softener fills the fibers and kills absorbency permanently; heat melts the polyester border before it hurts the pile.

Can I use the same towel on wheels?

No. Brake dust is iron and it stays in the fibers no matter how carefully you launder. Keep one towel for paint, one for glass, and something expendable for wheels and door shuts.

Also considered

  • Chamois and synthetic chamois — They squeegee rather than absorb, which means the last of the grit is pushed along the panel rather than lifted into a pile. Microfiber replaced them for a reason.
  • Marketplace towels advertising 1600 gsm for a few dollars — No maker page, no consistent measurement, and the GSM figure is unverifiable. When a number has no source, it is a decoration.
  • Household bath towels — Cotton loops trap grit and hold it at the surface, and the weave is hard enough to mark clear coat. This is the single most common cause of the swirls people blame on the wash.
  • Drying aids sold as towel replacements — A quick-detail spray makes drying safer and faster, but it is used with a towel, not instead of one.
  • Air blowers for home use — Genuinely the safest way to dry a car and a reasonable upgrade later, but a leaf blower on a dusty driveway is a way of sandblasting the paint you just washed.
