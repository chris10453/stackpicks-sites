How to shop for a clay bar

Published weight or dimensions
Adam's prints 80 g for the Visco bar and 2.5 × 1.5 × 4.25 in for the Clay Block. Chemical Guys prints 100 g. Mothers prints 80 g and 100 g bars depending on the kit. A bar with no published weight is a guess about how many cars it covers.
How many times you can reuse it
This is the real running cost. A bar is done when it is dropped; Adam's rates the Clay Mitt at 20 to 40 uses; the rubber pads rinse clean under a tap. Over three years the reusable options cost a fraction of what bars do.
Grade, and whether the maker admits it marks paint
Adam's says outright that the Clay Mitt is a medium grade that can leave light micro-marring and is best used before a machine polish. That honesty is worth more than a softer claim, because marring is exactly what the aggressive options do.
Whether a lubricant comes with it
Clay dragged over dry paint scratches it. Mothers sells a California Gold Clay Kit variant that packs a 16 oz instant detailer with the bar; Adam's sells its bars in combination with Detail Spray. Buying the clay alone and forgetting the lubricant is the classic first mistake.
Recovery after a drop
A traditional bar picks up driveway grit and has to be binned. A foam-backed block or a mitt can be rinsed under a tap and carried on. Adam's built the Clay Block around exactly that failure, which is why it is on this page.
What else it works on
Five of the six products here are rated by their makers for glass, chrome or smooth plastics as well as paint, which matters because bonded fallout on a windshield is what causes the wiper judder people blame on the blades.

Questions people ask

How do I know if my car needs claying?

Wash and dry it, then put your hand in a thin plastic bag and run it lightly over the paint. Bonded fallout that your eyes cannot see feels like fine sandpaper through the bag. If the paint feels like glass, skip claying and go straight to protection.

Can I use a clay bar without lubricant?

No. Clay dragged across dry paint marks it immediately. Use a detailer spray or a dedicated clay lubricant and keep the surface wet the whole time — Mothers sells a Clay Kit variant with a 16 oz instant detailer for exactly this reason, and Adam's recommends its Detail Spray with the Clay Mitt.

How often should a car be clayed?

Once or twice a year for a car parked outside, less for a garaged one. It is a preparation step rather than a maintenance step: you clay before you wax, seal or coat, because protection applied over bonded fallout locks the fallout in.

Does clay remove scratches?

No. Clay removes what is stuck on top of the clear coat — rail dust, overspray, tree sap, brake dust. Scratches are in the clear coat and need polishing. Adam's is explicit that its medium-grade mitt can even add light marring that a polish then removes.

What happens if I drop the clay bar?

Bin it. A bar that has touched the ground has picked up grit that will be dragged across your paint on the next pass. That single failure is why the reusable options in this guide exist and why the Adam's bar ships with a tool to hold it.

Is a clay mitt better than a clay bar?

Faster, not gentler. Adam's rates its mitt at 20 to 40 uses and says it can leave light micro-marring, which is fine before a machine polish and not fine as a final step. A light-duty bar is the safer choice if the car is not going to be polished afterwards.

Also considered

  • Heavy-duty and aggressive-grade clay bars — Sold for neglected paint and industrial fallout, and they mar clear coat on the way through. A regularly washed car wants light or medium, and the heavier grades commit you to a machine polish afterwards.
  • Clay towels sold without a grade or a reuse figure — The polymer-coated towels are a real category, but the ones reaching marketplaces publish neither an abrasive grade nor how many washes the coating survives, so there is nothing to hold the seller to.
  • All-in-one clay and coating products — Combining decontamination with protection means the protection goes on while the fallout is still being lifted. The two steps want different lubricants and different pressures, and doing them separately takes about twenty minutes longer.
  • Kitchen substitutes and modeling clay — Automotive detailing clay is a graded abrasive suspended in a specific polymer. Craft clay has neither the grade nor the release properties, and the failure mode is a paint correction you did not budget for.
