Gifts for cat owners have a specific failure mode. The human likes it, the cat ignores it, and the human owns a large object they feel guilty about. Every pick below is judged on two things gift guides skip: the footprint in inches, and whether the part that wears out can be bought again.

On a small budget go straight to the Necoichi scratcher bowl. If they rent and have no floor to spare, the Tab is the one. If the litter box is the problem there are two answers here: a better box, or furniture to hide it.

How to shop for a gift for a cat owner

Footprint in inches, not "compact"
Cat furniture is bought online and delivered into apartments where it does not fit. Footprints here run from a 6.5-inch wall board to a 20 × 20 inch base, and that decides whether it stays out.
Can you buy the wear part again
Every scratcher is a consumable with a frame around it. Some brands here sell replacement pads, filters or carpet; the rest expect you to buy the whole object again.
Published load rating
If a cat lands on it from height, the number has to exist. Ratings here run from 20 lb on a carpeted tower to 44 lb on an enclosure top, and four makers print no figure at all.
Whether it looks like cat equipment
A living room holds only so much beige carpet, which is why hidden litter furniture sells. Half the picks here are meant to be mistaken for furniture, and that is why they get kept.

Questions people ask

What is the safest gift for a cat owner whose cat I have never met?

The Necoichi scratcher bowl. No assembly, about the space of a large dinner plate, and if the cat ignores it nobody is stuck with a large object. Reversible construction means one bowl is effectively two.

Do cats prefer horizontal or vertical scratchers?

Both, and individual cats have strong preferences. With a gift, add an option rather than replace a habit: if there is already an upright post, the Necoichi bowl adds to it.

How long does an automatic feeder run without power?

The PETLIBRO Granary keeps its schedule on three alkaline D cells when mains power fails. What stops is the Wi-Fi, so remote feeding is unavailable until power returns even though the meals continue.

How many litter boxes should a household have?

One per cat plus one, per Ohio State's Indoor Pet Initiative, and one on every floor the cat uses. Hiding a box does not fix a house that is short of boxes.
