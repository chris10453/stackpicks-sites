A cat does three things to a sofa. It sheds on it, it occasionally throws up on it, and it works the corner of the arm with its claws. Covers solve the first two completely and the third not at all, and no maker on this page will tell you that in writing.

So read this as two products in one category. A quilted protector with straps takes the hair and the accident and goes in the washing machine. A stretch or woven slipcover takes the room back from a sofa that is already scratched. Buy the first if the sofa is new, the second if it is not, and buy a scratching post either way — nothing here stops claws.

How to shop for a couch cover

A published fit measurement
Sizes named "sofa" and "loveseat" are not specifications; furniture varies by a foot. All five here print inches somewhere, but only four print a figure for every size they sell — the reversible protector gives one measurement for a range of fourteen.
What is actually in the layers
A waterproof barrier, an absorbent top layer and plain quilted polyester behave completely differently when a cat is sick at four in the morning. One of these holds a stated 40 cups of liquid; one blocks it outright; three simply cover.
Whether it stays put
A cover a cat can rearrange in one leap is a cover you straighten daily. Straps with real hooks, silicone grip dots and elastic hems each solve part of this; a decorative throw solves none of it.
Whether it still looks like furniture
This site is about cat gear that does not announce itself. A quilted pet protector says pet protector across the room; a woven jacquard slipcover with tassels reads as a deliberate decision about the sofa.

Questions people ask

Will a couch cover stop my cat scratching the sofa?

No. Not one of these five makers claims it will, and a woven or knitted cover gives claws something better to catch than upholstery. A cover protects against hair, spills and wear. The scratching is solved with a post beside the sofa corner the cat has chosen — our scratcher guide covers the shapes that work.

What size couch cover do I need?

Measure the sofa across the back at its widest point and from the front edge of the seat over the cushions to the back. Gorilla Grip's waterproof sizes run from a 76 by 67 inch chair to an 82 by 122 inch oversized sofa; H.VERSAILTEX's woven sizes run 71 by 91 up to 91 by 134. Both size charts are published, so the tape answers the question.

Waterproof or absorbent — which is better for a cat?

Waterproof, if the cat is sick or has litter box accidents, because a barrier layer stops liquid reaching the cushion at all. Absorbent is better for the ordinary household, where the issue is a spilled drink and a lot of hair; the plush cover here holds a tested 40 cups.

Do these covers stay in place?

The two Sofa Shield protectors use two-inch straps with zinc alloy hooks, the plush cover uses a silicone-dot underside on a fabric seat, and the stretch cover uses an elastic hem. The woven cotton cover uses nothing, which is why it is the one you will straighten daily.

Can I put a cover over a sofa my cat already scratched?

That is the best use for the two H.VERSAILTEX covers. A stretch jacquard pulls flat over damaged upholstery and a woven throw hides an arm entirely. Neither will stop the damage continuing underneath, so pair it with a scratching post and, if the corner is the target, a post that is at least as tall as the cat's full stretch.

Also considered

  • Double-sided anti-scratch tape — Sold under several names, with no manufacturer site behind any of them that publishes a specification. The tape works while it is sticky, which is days, and it leaves residue on upholstery. A scratching post placed at the exact corner the cat uses is the durable answer.
  • Clear vinyl scratch guards — The clear plastic panels sold for sofa corners do stop claws, and they look like plastic panels taped to a sofa. In a small flat the sofa is most of the room, which is the whole reason this site exists.
  • Marketplace couch covers with no brand — The largest group in this category by volume, and unusable here — no maker page, no published fit measurement and no stated construction, so there is nothing to compare beyond photographs.
  • Furniture covers sold by seat-cushion count — A cover sold for a "3 cushion couch" tells you nothing about the sofa's width, and two three-cushion sofas can differ by eighteen inches. Buy from a maker that publishes inches.
