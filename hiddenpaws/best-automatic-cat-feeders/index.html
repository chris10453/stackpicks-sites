Every feeder works in the shop. What matters is 4 a.m. on day two of your trip, when the power blinked or the kibble bridged over the outlet. So the manuals were read for four things: what runs on battery, whether the schedule survives a Wi-Fi outage, how the mechanism clears a jam, and whether it can be anchored.

The answers are less reassuring than the advertising. Two brands publish nothing about the schedule continuing while the internet is down, and appear here with that gap named. Two more shut the Wi-Fi off on battery. One sidesteps it with no radio, no app and no cord: three AA cells, a published twelve months.

How to shop for an automatic cat feeder

What the backup battery actually does
The word covers everything from 24 hours on a built-in cell to 58 hours on four C cells to 12 months on three AA cells. Cell type matters too — three brands specify alkaline and warn against rechargeables.
Whether the schedule runs offline
Only Whisker puts offline operation in its feature list; PETLIBRO documents it in an FAQ. PetSafe and Catit publish nothing saying the schedule keeps dispensing while the internet is down, so nothing here claims it.
How the mechanism handles a jam
Augers bridge and impellers stick; conveyors and carousels do neither. Catit publishes four anti-jam mechanisms, PetSafe uses a conveyor, and the tray feeders have nothing to jam.
Whether a cat can defeat it
A stainless bowl in a holder, an enclosed path, a tamper-resistant lid, or a base you screw to a board. One feeder here is built to be fastened down, the only answer that always works.
Wet food, or dry only
Only two feeders here take wet food, and they differ — a thermoelectric cooler rated for a published 72 hours, or twin ice packs in a carousel with no electronics.

Questions people ask

How many meals a day should an automatic feeder give a cat?

Cornell's Feline Health Center says that once a cat is an adult, at about one year, once or twice a day suits most cases, and kittens under six months do best on three. Every feeder here covers that — the ceilings run from five meals to twelve.

What happens to my feeder in a power cut?

It depends on the model, which is the point of this guide. The published answers run from 12 hours on three AA cells, through about 58 hours on four C cells, to a year on a feeder with no mains connection. Two brands also shut the Wi-Fi off on battery: meals continue, the app goes quiet.

Do automatic feeders work with wet food?

Two of the six here do. One cools trays thermoelectrically for a published 72 hours; the other uses twin ice packs in a carousel. The FDA's advice is to refrigerate or discard leftover canned food promptly, which is why an uncooled dry-food feeder is the wrong machine for it.

What kibble size can these feeders handle?

Between 2 mm and 15 mm for the PETLIBRO vane, up to 9 mm for the Catit impeller, up to three quarters of an inch for the PetSafe conveyor. Measure your kibble first: a feeder that cannot pass your food jams on day one.
