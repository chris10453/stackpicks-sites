A window perch is the cheapest square foot of cat territory in an apartment. It uses no floor, drills no holes, and it puts the cat where Ohio State's Indoor Pet Initiative says a cat wants to be, which is somewhere raised with a view of what it can already hear and smell.

Fair warning about this page: three of the five picks are K&H, and that is not laziness. K&H is the only brand in the category that publishes a suction rating at all, and a suction rating is the single specification that decides whether the product is furniture or an incident. The other two picks are here because they solve problems suction cannot.

How to shop for a cat window perch

Published suction rating
The number that matters and the number almost nobody prints. K&H publishes 50 lb across its EZ Mount line; the Kitty Cot brand quotes 12 lb per cup across four cups. Everyone else sells "strong suction cups" and hopes.
The glass space it needs
A perch that needs 27 inches of uninterrupted pane will not go on a window divided by muntins at 20 inches. Measure the glass, not the window frame, and measure it before anything else.
Frame material
Steel frames hold shape under a cat that flops; plastic and PVC frames flex and eventually take a set. Every K&H perch here is a steel frame; the differences are in the shelf above it.
Whether the cover washes
This is the piece of cat furniture that collects the most fur and the most sun. A removable machine-washable pad is the difference between three years and one.
Cover or no cover
An open shelf is a sunbathing spot. A pod or a hooded perch is a hiding place with a view, which the AAFP and ISFM guidelines describe as a safe place — private, secure, raised. Nervous cats use the second kind and ignore the first.

Questions people ask

How much weight can a cat window perch hold?

K&H publishes 50 lb across its EZ Mount range, which covers any domestic cat several times over. The number to distrust is the unpublished one, because a mount that survives a 9 lb cat sitting down can still fail under the same cat arriving at speed.

How much glass does a window perch need?

Between 11 and 31 inches of clear height depending on the model, and 27 inches of width for every K&H perch here. Measure the uninterrupted pane, not the window opening — a horizontal divider in the middle of the glass rules out everything on this page.

Will suction cups damage the window or the paint?

They sit on the glass rather than the frame, so there is nothing to damage and nothing to fill in when you leave, which is the entire reason this is the renter's answer. Clean the glass and the cups monthly, as K&H instructs, or they release.

Do window perches work on double glazing?

Yes, on the inner pane, the same as on single glazing. The variable that matters is temperature — a very cold pane and a warm room stress the seal, so winter is when a neglected cup lets go.
