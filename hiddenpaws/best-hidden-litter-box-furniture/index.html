How to shop for hidden litter box furniture

Interior width, not exterior width
A 31.5-inch cabinet can have a 19.8-inch compartment once you account for panel thickness and a divider. Measure your pan, add two inches a side, and compare against the interior figure only.
Entry opening in inches
The spec brands hide. Yaheetech publishes 6.5 x 8 inches on one model and a 10.6-inch cat hole on another, and that difference decides whether a 14 lb cat folds through or stops using the box.
Rated load on top
If the cabinet is going to be a side table, the number has to cover a lamp, a plant and a cat landing on it. Published ratings in this set run from 22 lb to 176 lb, and one of them changes depending on whether the divider is installed.
Ventilation you can see
Covered boxes hold odour in — the consistent position of every welfare source cited at the bottom of this page. Vent holes in the back panel and a door that does not seal are the difference between a cabinet and a sealed box of ammonia.
Scooping access
Front doors are for the cat. You want a second opening — double doors, a lift-off top, a slide-out tray — because a cabinet you kneel and reach into is a cabinet you scoop less often.
Weight and assembly
The particleboard cabinets here run 27 to 36 lb and their makers quote 20 to 30 minutes to build. A rattan-and-steel frame at 11.5 lb is a different proposition in a fourth-floor walk-up.

Questions people ask

How much bigger than the litter box does an enclosure need to be?

Add two inches to each side of your pan's footprint and eight inches above its rim. A 17.5 x 20-inch pan wants an interior of roughly 20 x 23 inches, which is why the 19.8-inch compartments in this category are tighter than they sound.

Do covered litter boxes and enclosures make the smell worse?

They concentrate it. Ohio State's Indoor Pet Initiative states plainly that covered litter boxes concentrate odours more than uncovered boxes, and Cornell's feline behaviour guidance makes the same point: covers hold odours in. An enclosure moves the smell out of your nose and into a small box your cat has to stand in, so ventilation holes and daily scooping stop being optional.

Will my cat actually use an enclosed litter box?

Some will not. The ASPCA's litter box guidance is to remove covers and liners from all litter boxes, and a hood or liner that makes a cat uncomfortable is listed among the common causes of a cat eliminating outside the box. Buy the enclosure, keep the old open pan for two weeks, and watch which one gets used before you get rid of anything.

How wide does the entry hole need to be?

Wider than the published 6.5 inches on the narrowest cabinet here. Openings in this category run from 6.5 x 8 inches up to a 10.6-inch-high cat hole, and the taller openings are the ones that suit a cat over about 12 lb, a long-haired cat, or an older cat that no longer folds.

Can I put a self-cleaning box inside a cabinet?

Only if the interior clears the machine in all three dimensions plus its lid travel. A Litter-Robot 4 alone has a 22 x 27-inch footprint and a 29.5-inch height, which is larger than every interior in this guide, so the answer for that machine specifically is no.
