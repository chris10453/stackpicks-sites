About Hidden Paws

Hidden Paws is an independent buying guide for people who live with cats in places that are not big enough for cat furniture to be ugly. I am Chris Y., and I write every guide here. Two cats, one bedroom, one hallway where the litter box has to go.

The guides compare the things a product page hides: interior dimensions rather than exterior ones, door openings measured in inches, how high a post really stands once it is assembled, what a feeder does at 4 a.m. when the power blinks. Those are the numbers that decide whether a thing fits your apartment and your cat, and they are the numbers most cat-gear roundups skip.

How a guide gets made

Every specification on this site comes from the manufacturer's own published page, manual, or spec sheet, and each guide lists its sources at the bottom so you can check them. Where a brand does not publish a number, the guide says so instead of guessing. Nothing here is copied from a retailer's bullet points.

We do not accept payment for placement. Brands cannot see a guide before it publishes, cannot buy an award, and cannot change a ranking. We earn a commission when you buy through a link (see the affiliate disclosure), and that commission is identical whichever pick you choose, which is exactly why the ranking can stay honest.

What we do not do

We do not print prices, because a price typed into a page is wrong within a week; the retailer's page is the only honest source for what something costs today. We do not print star ratings or review counts. And we never present desk research as hands-on testing. Every guide on this site today is built from published specifications, manuals and welfare guidance, and each one says so in "How we picked"; when a product has been bought and lived with, the guide will say that instead.

Corrections are welcome and get fixed the same week: stackpicksmedia@gmail.com.
