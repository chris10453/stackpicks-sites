The toys that keep an indoor cat busy are not the ones that need you on the other end. A wand is the best fifteen minutes of a cat's day and it stops the moment you put it down. The toys on this page carry on working while you are at the desk, on a call, or asleep.

All six do the same basic thing — they make the cat use its paws and its nose to get at food — and they differ in two ways that matter in a small flat: how much floor they occupy permanently, and whether they hold dry kibble, wet food or only treats. Four of these makers publish a footprint. Two publish nothing at all, which is a real problem when the toy has to live between a sofa and a radiator.

How to shop for a puzzle toy for an indoor cat

Does it work without you
A puzzle a cat can solve alone is enrichment; a wand is exercise you supervise. This page is about the first kind, because the problem it solves is the eight hours when nobody is home.
Published footprint
Anything that lives on the floor of a one-bedroom flat has to earn its square foot. The published sizes here run from 7.6 inches square up to 16.4, and two makers do not publish a figure at all.
What it holds
A toy that takes the cat's ordinary dinner turns feeding into foraging at no extra cost. A toy that only takes treats becomes a calorie problem if it is used daily.
Whether the difficulty can move
Cats solve puzzles and then ignore them. Adjustable openings, removable pieces and multiple levels are what stop a twenty-dollar toy becoming a twenty-dollar shelf ornament by Thursday.

Questions people ask

How long will a puzzle toy keep a cat busy?

Minutes at a time, several times a day, which is what a cat's natural hunting pattern looks like. A baited maze or food tree is usually worked in bursts of five to fifteen minutes and returned to when the cat is next hungry. Nothing on this page occupies a cat for an hour in one go, and any product claiming otherwise is selling.

Can I feed a whole meal through a puzzle?

Through some of them. The Catit Senses 2.0 Food Tree holds a full dry ration, the Cat Amazing maze takes kibble, and the multi feeder takes wet food. The activity board and the ball track are treat-and-chase toys, so daily use with treats adds calories you have to subtract from the bowl.

My cat ignores puzzle toys. What now?

Make it easier and make it smell of dinner. Start with the widest opening or the shallowest compartment, bait it with the food the cat already eats, and leave it where the cat sleeps rather than where you want it to live. Most refusals are a difficulty problem, not a cat problem, which is why the adjustable toys here score higher.

Are these safe to leave out unsupervised?

The food toys yes, with the usual caveat about a cat that chews plastic. The ball track publishes a safety bar across the middle for exactly this reason. Cardboard toys get shredded eventually, and a cat that eats the cardboard rather than the food needs a plastic one instead.

How many toys does one indoor cat need?

Two categories, not two objects — one food-based puzzle it can work alone and one thing to chase. The veterinary environmental guidance asks for opportunities to play and to express predatory behavior, and a rotation of two or three items beats a basket of fifteen the cat has stopped noticing.

Also considered

  • Doc & Phoebe's Indoor Hunting Feeder — The vet-designed feeder we most wanted to include, and the maker's website returned an error to every automated request we made, so no specification could be read from the source. We do not print numbers taken from a retailer.
  • Laser pointers — A laser gives a cat a chase with no catch at the end, which is the one thing predatory play is supposed to provide. If you use one, finish every session by landing the dot on a physical toy or a few pieces of kibble.
  • Battery-powered automatic wands — They work until the cat learns the pattern, which takes about a week, and then they are a noise in the corner. The food puzzles here stay interesting because the reward is real.
  • Treat balls with no adjustable opening — One difficulty setting means one week of interest. Every pick above either has adjustable openings, several mechanisms, or enough capacity to be a feeding system rather than a novelty.
