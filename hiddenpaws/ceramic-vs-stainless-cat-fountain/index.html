Both materials beat plastic for the same reason, and it is not the material itself. Plastic scratches, and a scratched surface holds the film that a wipe does not remove. Glazed ceramic and 304 stainless do not scratch in normal use, which is why vets asking about a cat's chin ask about the bowl.

After that, the two diverge on three practical axes: what you can put in a dishwasher, what the thing weighs when full, and whether the maker will tell you how loud it is. Nobody publishes all three, so this page is about which gap you can live with.

How to shop for a cat water fountain

What goes in the dishwasher
PetSafe says the whole Pagoda goes on the top rack and the pump is hand-washed. PETLIBRO names a temperature — below 194F for the stainless tray and the filter tray, hand wash below 122F for everything else. Ceramic wins the simplicity test; stainless wins the precision test.
Published noise
PETLIBRO publishes 30 dB or lower. PetSafe describes the Pagoda's pump as whisper-quiet and submersible and publishes no figure. In a one-bedroom flat where the fountain runs all night, a number beats an adjective.
Weight and breakability
The stainless fountain is 1.6 lb empty. A ceramic fountain of similar capacity is several times that and it breaks if it lands on a tiled floor. That matters for refilling, for carrying it to the sink, and for moving flat.
Published dimensions
PETLIBRO publishes 10.8 by 8.1 by 4.7 inches. PetSafe publishes a 70 oz capacity for the Pagoda and no dimensions at all on the product page, which is an odd gap on an object you have to find floor space for.
Flow control
The Pagoda's flow is listed as non-adjustable — two free-falling streams, take them or leave them. That is fine for a cat that likes moving water and unhelpful for one that finds a stream alarming.

Questions people ask

Is ceramic or stainless steel better for a cat fountain?

Both beat plastic and the reason is scratching rather than the material. Ceramic is easier to clean thoroughly because the whole bowl goes in the dishwasher; stainless is lighter, unbreakable and, in this case, the only one with a published noise figure. Pick on cleaning habit and noise tolerance, not on hygiene theory.

Do ceramic cat fountains break easily?

They break the way a mug breaks — not in use, but on the day it meets a tiled floor. The stainless fountain here weighs 1.6 lb empty and dents instead. If the fountain lives somewhere it gets knocked, that is the deciding fact.

Which is quieter, ceramic or stainless?

Only one maker publishes a number, and it is the stainless one — 30 dB or lower. Most fountain noise comes from a stream falling into a half-empty reservoir rather than from the pump, so the fastest way to quieten any fountain of either material is to keep it topped up.

Can you put a cat fountain in the dishwasher?

Parts of both. PetSafe says the Pagoda goes on the top rack and the pump is hand-washed. PETLIBRO says the stainless tray and filter tray tolerate below 194F and everything else should be hand washed below 122F. No pump from either brand is dishwasher safe.

How often should a fountain be cleaned?

Weekly for the bowl and every filter change for the pump, which for PETLIBRO is a fortnight. The material only decides how easy that is; it does not shorten the schedule.
