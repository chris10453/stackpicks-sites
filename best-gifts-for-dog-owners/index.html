Gifts for dog owners fail in two ways. Anything that has to fit the dog (a harness, a bed, a coat) comes back when the guess is wrong. Anything that has to suit the dog (a toy, a chew) lands in a basket after one afternoon. The five picks below are chosen to avoid both: one is sized for the human, one is sized by a printed capacity, and the toy is a puzzle shape rather than a squeaker.

On a small budget go to the LickiMat Classic Buddy, the cheapest thing here and the one that does a job a bowl cannot. For a dog that already has every toy, the Ruffwear Basecamp Bowl is the upgrade they will not buy for themselves.

How to shop for a gift for a dog owner

Does it need the dog's measurements
A gift that needs a chest girth or a weight is a guess. Three of the five picks come in one size (the pouch, the toy and the mat), and the two bowls are sold in sizes with the capacity printed in cups.
A number the maker prints
Dimensions, capacities and care instructions are the only way to judge a gift you cannot hold. Every pick here has at least one measurement on the maker's own page, and nothing in the table is filled in from a guess.
Used daily or abandoned
A treat pouch and a bowl are touched every day. A toy competes with every other toy in the house. Picks that earn a daily slot rank above picks that depend on the dog's mood.
Can it be washed
Anything that touches food or a dog's mouth gets dirty fast. Three picks here are dishwasher safe by their makers' own care notes, and the pouch and the mat are hand wash only.
