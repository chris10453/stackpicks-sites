How a StackPicks guide works

We start from the buyer's constraint (a 24-inch counter, a one-bedroom apartment, a garage with no wall left) and rank against that. Every guide has a short list at the top, a comparison table, the products we rejected and why, and a named editor you can email.

Every number we print is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page. Where a brand publishes nothing for a row, the table shows a dash rather than a figure borrowed from a retail description — and the pick with the gap says so in its reasons to avoid.
