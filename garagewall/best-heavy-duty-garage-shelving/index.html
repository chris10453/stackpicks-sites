Three different numbers are sold as "capacity" here and they are not comparable — per shelf, total, and total-but-only-on-the-leveling-feet. A unit titled "2,000 lbs Capacity" holds 400 pounds a shelf. Across the units checked the per-shelf spread runs 400 to 2,500 pounds, and two brands publish what happens to the rating once the unit rolls: it falls to 500 pounds for the whole rack.

Shelving is the only storage on this site that needs nothing from your building: no joists, no studs, no anchors. If your garage has plaster, steel studs, masonry walls or trusses you do not trust, a free-standing unit is the answer and an overhead rack is not.

How to shop for garage shelving

Per-shelf capacity, with its qualifier
Seville publishes 800 pounds a shelf "on leveling feet" and 500 pounds total on its own casters — same unit, same box, a factor of eight apart.
Total vs per-shelf
VEVOR's title says 2,000 pounds; its spec list says 400 a shelf across five shelves. Both are true, and only one tells you what a single shelf takes.
Deck material
Wire lets dust through and takes small items badly; particleboard sags if it gets wet; laminate wipes clean. Pick for what you store, not for the photo.
Footprint against a real wall
24 inches deep is standard and eats two feet of bay. A 12.6-inch unit holds far less but leaves a walkway, which in a one-car garage is the whole decision.
Boltless or bolted
Rivet-lock and snap-together units go up in under an hour with a mallet; bolt-together units take an evening and hold alignment better loaded.
Anti-tip and leveling
Garage floors slope toward the door by design, so leveling feet are not a luxury on a tall loaded rack. Trinity ships an anti-tip kit as standard; Sakugi includes a wall fixing kit.

Questions people ask

How much weight can garage shelving hold?

Per shelf the published figures run 400 pounds (VEVOR) to 2,000 (Gladiator), with 800 to 1,000 the common band, while totals run 1,000 to 8,000. Always check which of the two a brand is quoting — VEVOR's title says 2,000 pounds and that is the total across five shelves.

What does "evenly distributed load" actually mean?

It means the rating assumes weight spread across the whole shelf, not concentrated in the middle or at one end. Gladiator publishes 1,000 pounds a shelf on the EZ Connect rack without the phrase; Seville and Trinity attach "on leveling feet" instead, which is the same idea applied to the frame rather than the deck.

Does putting shelving on casters reduce its capacity?

Dramatically. Seville publishes 4,000 pounds total for its 48 × 24-inch unit on leveling feet and 500 on its own wheels, a factor of eight; Trinity publishes 1,350 and 500 for its rolling rack. Two brands, same admission. If you need real capacity, use the feet.

How deep should garage shelving be?

24 inches is the standard here and it costs two feet of bay, which is usually too much in a single-car garage — a 12.6-inch unit holds far less but leaves a walkway. Measure from the wall to the widest point of your car door when open, not to the car.

Do I need to anchor a shelving unit to the wall?

It is cheap insurance on anything 72 inches tall, and a garage floor slopes toward the door by design, so level the feet first and anchor second. Trinity's PRO ships an anti-tip kit as standard and Sakugi includes a wall fixing kit; the rest leave it to you.
