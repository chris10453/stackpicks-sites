A one-car garage is roughly 12 feet wide and a car is about 6, so the bench has to live in a 3-foot strip beside a vehicle or across the end wall. That rules out the 6-foot benches most guides open with, and it makes depth and height matter more than any capacity figure.

Height is the specification nobody checks and everybody regrets. A fixed 34-inch bench is a compromise between standing work and precision work; a bench that travels from 27.5 to 40.8 inches is a table saw outfeed one weekend and a soldering desk the next. Two of the five here adjust, one folds flat to 10 inches deep, and one rolls out of the way entirely.

How to shop for a garage workbench

Footprint against a 3-foot strip
In a 12-foot bay with a 6-foot car, anything deeper than 25 inches stops a car door opening. Every depth below is the manufacturer's own figure, not an estimate.
Published top capacity, distributed
Gladiator publishes 3,000 pounds distributed on the hardwood top, Seville 1,000 and 500 on its two benches, and the Foldaway 200. "Distributed" is doing real work in all four numbers.
Adjustable height, and its range
27.5 to 40.8 inches covers seated precision work and standing assembly. A fixed-height bench is only right for one of those jobs, and you find out which after you buy it.
Power at the bench
A garage bench with no outlet becomes an extension-cord problem. Seville's workcenter publishes a six-outlet strip with two USB ports and a 5.5-foot cord; the others publish nothing, which means a cord.
Can it get out of the way
The Foldaway closes to 10 inches deep against the wall and the Mobile Workstation rolls. In a garage that also holds a car, disappearing is a feature no capacity number replaces.

Questions people ask

What size workbench fits a one-car garage?

Four feet wide and 20 to 25 inches deep. A 12-foot bay with a 6-foot car leaves about 3 feet each side, and a 25-inch bench keeps roughly 11 inches of walking room — enough to open a car door part way.

What height should a garage workbench be?

Between 34 and 38 inches for standing work, and around 30 inches if you work seated. The Gladiator bench here adjusts from 27.5 to 40.8 inches and the Seville from 30 to 39, which is why both suit a garage that does more than one kind of job.

How much weight can a garage workbench hold?

From 200 to 3,000 published pounds on this page. The wall-mounted Foldaway lists 200 pounds, the lighted workcenter 500, the rolling workstation 680, the 48-inch Seville 1,000 and the Gladiator hardwood bench 3,000 distributed.

Is a hardwood or steel workbench top better for a garage?

Hardwood is kinder to tools and takes a beating, but it absorbs solvent and needs sealing — Gladiator warrants its top for one year against a lifetime on the legs. Stainless, like the Foldaway's 1.5-inch top, cleans up after brake fluid and never needs finishing.

Do I need a workbench with outlets?

You need power at the bench; whether it is built in is a price question. The Seville workcenter publishes six outlets, two USB ports and a 5.5-foot cord. Bolting a power strip to a bare bench does the same job for less, as long as it is a strip rated for the tools you plug into it.

Can a workbench go on a sloped garage floor?

Yes, if it has leveling feet. Garage slabs slope toward the door for drainage, and both Seville benches here publish adjustable leveling feet — the Seville workcenter's are padded. A bench without them rocks under hand pressure.
