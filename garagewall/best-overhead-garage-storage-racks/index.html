The ceiling is the only square footage nobody is using, and the only place a bad purchase can land on a car. Across nine racks the published capacities vary by a factor of six — 150 to 1,000 pounds — and three brands publish a joist-spacing requirement. The rest leave you to find out on the ladder.

Measure first: ceiling height, the lowest point of the opener rail, and the roof of whatever you park. Those three numbers decide this purchase more than any capacity figure — and if the answer is eight feet, skip to the HyLoft. Full walkthrough in how to plan a garage wall.

How to shop for an overhead garage rack

The capacity number, and its qualifier
"1,000 lbs evenly distributed" (Gladiator) and "250lb combined" (MonsterRAX, deck not included) are not the same claim. Every figure below keeps the manufacturer's wording.
Ceiling drop range
A rack dropping 22 to 40 inches takes up to 3 ft 4 in of headroom. In an 8-foot garage that leaves 56 inches under the deck — a rack you duck under, not park under.
What it says about your joists
FLEXIMOUNTS publishes a rule for joists over 24 inches apart; SafeRacks and NewAge state wood joists only, no steel or concrete. Brands that publish nothing here generate the returns.
Is a deck included
MonsterRAX ships a frame and tells you to supply 3/8-inch plywood; Gladiator builds the decking in. That changes the price band and the capacity.
Hardware you can count
Two of nine brands publish a parts list; HyLoft itemises 8 lag screws and 20 hex bolts. The rest describe fasteners in adjectives.
A warranty with a number on it
Gladiator publishes 10 years; SafeRacks, MonsterRAX, HyLoft and StoreYourBoard lifetime limited terms; FLEXIMOUNTS none at all.

Questions people ask

How much weight can an overhead garage rack hold?

Between 150 and 750 pounds across the six picks — the table keeps each brand's qualifier. The two 4 × 8 wire-deck racks sit at 600 and 750. The rating covers the rack, not your ceiling.

Will an overhead rack fit an 8-foot garage ceiling?

Only a shallow one. A 22 to 24-inch minimum drop leaves about 72 to 74 inches of headroom — less than the 75.3-inch roof height Ford publishes for a 2026 F-150 SuperCrew — while the HyLoft's 17-inch drop leaves roughly 79.

What joist spacing do these racks need?

FLEXIMOUNTS Classic fits any spacing at or under 24 inches on center, plus 48; the GL1 lists 12, 16 and 24. SafeRacks and NewAge require wood joists and exclude steel, I-beams and concrete.

Can I hang a rack from garage roof trusses?

Ask a professional first. The code's attic live load for limited storage is 10 pounds per square foot — across a 4 × 8 rack that is 320 pounds, less than half what most of these racks are rated to hold.

How often should I check the bolts on a ceiling rack?

At least once a year — Knape & Vogt write that into the HyLoft warranty conditions. Seasonal temperature swings in an uninsulated garage work fasteners loose.
