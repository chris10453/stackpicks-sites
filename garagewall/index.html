How these guides are made

Every capacity, dimension, hole pitch and warranty term on this site was copied from the manufacturer's own product page, spec table or installation manual. Not from a retailer, not from a search snippet, not from another buying guide. Where a brand publishes nothing, the guide says "not published" instead of filling the gap — and in this category that happens constantly, which is itself the most useful thing a buyer can know.

Three rules follow from that, and they are the whole method:

  • The qualifier travels with the number. "800 lb per shelf evenly distributed", "4,000 lb total on leveling feet", "500 lb total on wheels" and "250 lb combined, decking not included" are four different claims. They appear here exactly as the manufacturer wrote them.
  • When a brand and a retailer disagree, the brand's number wins and the conflict gets printed. That happened twice in the overhead-rack guide alone.
  • No prices, no star ratings, no review counts. Retailers change prices hourly and a stale price is worse than none; the retailer's own page is the only honest source. Guides show a price band instead.
